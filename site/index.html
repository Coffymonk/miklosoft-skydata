miklosoft-skydata

Cloud and rain maps for Europe, made every 15 minutes from open satellite, radar and forecast data, for the Radar tab of the Sky planner at miklosoft.se.

Files: manifest.json (times, sources, credits, accuracy) and tiles/{set}/{x}_{y}.bin.gz. How it works: the repository.
